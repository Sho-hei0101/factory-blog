Welcome to **factory-blog**, a minimal Next.js blog that reads Markdown from the filesystem.

## What you get

- App Router pages for home, tags, and individual posts.
- Frontmatter-driven metadata and CTA buttons.
- SEO basics (robots.txt and sitemap.xml).

Ready to publish? Add another post with the `npm run new:post` script.
